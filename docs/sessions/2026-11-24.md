## Decisions

- **Tailscale on the host, no port forwards** (ADR 0008). Forwarding 22 or 8006 would
  put a root login in front of internet-wide scanners.
- **No unattended GPU load test.** The test exists to observe a 13-year-old PSU under
  strain; a trip or failure can't be watched or recovered remotely.
- **No host reboots when working remotely.** The NVIDIA boot-trigger fix waits for
  on-site access. Container-level work (passthrough, `nvidia-smi` inside) is acceptable.
