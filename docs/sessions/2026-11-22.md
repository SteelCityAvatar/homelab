## Findings

- **CT 200 is on the LAN** at `192.168.1.164/24` (DHCP, not reserved). It also holds a
  global IPv6 address, so the router's IPv6 inbound policy matters (security item).
- **Device files exist but aren't created at boot.** `/dev/nvidia0`, `nvidiactl`,
  `nvidia-uvm`, `nvidia-uvm-tools` were made on demand by the first `nvidia-smi`
  (2026-11-11). No `nvidia-persistenced`, no NVIDIA modules-load or udev rules. After a
  reboot a container starting at boot would get no GPU. Fix planned:
  `/etc/modules-load.d/nvidia.conf` + a udev rule; verifying it needs a host reboot.
- **Major numbers:** `195` is NVIDIA's fixed major; `nvidia-uvm` (510) and
  `nvidia-caps` (235) are assigned at boot and may change — pass devices by path.
- ⚠️ Writing `/etc/modules-load.d/nvidia.conf` was proposed; not confirmed as run.
