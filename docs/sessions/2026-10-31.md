## Decision — replace the IronWolfs with 14TB recerts

| Option | Usable (mirrored) | $/TB (2026-10-28) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2× 4TB IronWolf (new) | 4TB | ~$50 | Quiet, new; holds only the irreplaceable subset |
| **2× Toshiba MG07ACA14TE 14TB** (recertified) | **14TB** | **~$25** | Fits all ~5TB including media; 2 bays stay free |
| 2× Exos X16 16TB (recertified) | 16TB | ~$29 | Alternative if priced right |
| Exos X10 10TB (refurb) | 10TB | ~$32 | Rejected: worse $/TB, ~2017 generation |

**Decided: swap to 2× 14TB.** Trade-offs accepted: louder and more power (7200rpm),
recertified rather than new — so **burn-in is mandatory**, inside the new drives'
return window. Prefer manufacturer-recertified with a stated warranty.

The IronWolfs serve as a temporary dry-run mirror until the new drives arrive, then
are wiped and returned within the retailer's 30-day window (≈ 2026-11-20).
